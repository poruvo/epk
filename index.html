Artist Bio

gon pulvo is the experimental electronic project of Stephon X. Jones, blending distorted rhythm, bass-heavy textures, horror atmosphere, and surreal narrative structure. His work crosses hip hop, house, ambient, jungle, and experimental bass while using recurring motifs, dreamlike tension, and reactive visuals to shape immersive live sets.

Read full bio ↓

gon pulvo composes and performs experimental electronic music live, crossing hip hop, house, ambient, and jungle. His sets fuse gritty sound design with narrative motifs and reactive visuals, creating immersive performance environments.

His recent work delves into psychological themes through recurring characters and looped dream sequences — unfolding across recorded releases and live shows. The Boogeyman project distills this approach into a narrative-driven sonic world exploring fear, identity, recursion, and dream logic.

With a fully mobile rig, he can perform in venues ranging from festival stages to DIY spaces, pop-up events, and gallery installations. The setup is designed for total flexibility without sacrificing performance depth.
