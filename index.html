Performance Format

gon pulvo performs electronic music live using 2x Dirtywave M8 units for real-time sequencing, arrangement, transitions, effects, and sound manipulation. Songs are actively performed and restructured during the set rather than simply played back as finished recordings. Audio-reactive visuals can be added when appropriate.

Read full bio ↓

gon pulvo is the experimental electronic project of Stephon X. Jones. His music moves between hip hop, house, ambient, jungle, bass music, and less easily categorized territory, with each track built around whatever musical language best fits the idea.

The project is centered on connection: between styles, sounds, people, experiences, and recurring ideas. Releases can move from dance music to abstract sound design, narrative work, rock, or experimental composition without treating genre as a fixed identity.

Live, Stephon performs and reshapes the catalog in real time using hardware sequencing and arrangement. Recent work includes the narrative-driven Boogeyman project alongside standalone releases and ongoing genre experiments.

Featured Project

Boogeyman Project

Boogeyman is a 15-track narrative-driven electronic project built around recurring characters, shifting perspectives, dream logic, identity, fear, and causality. Its story unfolds through both music and accompanying narrative material, with motifs and events recurring in altered forms throughout the project.
